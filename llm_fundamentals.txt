Large Language Models (LLMs) are deep learning models trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human language. Most modern LLMs use the Transformer architecture, introduced in the "Attention Is All You Need" paper.

LLMs excel at tasks like translation, text summarization, code generation, and question answering. However, LLMs can suffer from hallucination (generating incorrect or fabricated information) and lack access to private or real-time data.
